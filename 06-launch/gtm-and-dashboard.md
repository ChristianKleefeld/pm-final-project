## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, The main goal is currently not to make consumers aware of the portal - but making sure users starting an agreement plan will finish a sustainable plan (conversion - share of agreements)
- **Target audience:** Consumers with multiple open claims and variable income, who cannot pay a claim in one go

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Not relevant for all users, but for a specific user group with a specific problem as defined by the target audience

## Channels
1. **Owned: in-app (consumer portal) place the explainer at the income field (inline or expandable),**
2. **Owned: email (consumers who dropped out earlier) - is this even allowed? would they still have an open debt or maybe resolved it differently?**
3. **Paid: _(not filled in)_**

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Primary: plan completion rate (agreements finished ÷ started) in the target segment, baseline [41.6%], target +[5] pp by [+8 weeks], measured against a holdout.
Sustainability guardrail: share of completed plans with first installment paid on time and still active at day [60/90].
Leading indicators: drop-off at the income step, explainer open rate, income field completion, support contacts tagged “why do you need my income.”
Other guardrails: complaints, email opt-outs/unsubscribes, and signs of income under-reporting.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Pause and review copy/placement if, after [N = ~500] income-step sessions, drop-off at the income step has not improved by ≥[3] pp vs. holdout. Pause rollout if completion rises but day-30 first-installment miss rate rises by >[3] pp vs. holdout. Stop email if complaints or opt-outs exceed [0.5]% of sends, or on any regulator/legal complaint. Owner of the pause decision: [name].
