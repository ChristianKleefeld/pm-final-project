Future-state journey: a plan they understand and can keep

Variable-income consumers, about 34% of journey starters. German portal, BU Collection.

Goal: set up a payment plan they can realistically keep, so the collection letters stop and the debt does not grow. Move: from "get the consumer to a plan quickly" to "help the consumer choose a plan they understand and can keep".

  1. 1

    Orient

    User action

    Opens the portal on a break and sees all open claims in one combined total.

    Internal state

    Wary, short on time, unsure what is expected.

    Pain point addressed

    Claims shown separately; no stated purpose for the questions.

    T-202N-03T-206
    • Show all open claims in one total→no manual adding up.
    • State upfront why income is asked→less fear before the question.
  2. 2

    Share income

    User action

    Answers an income question with a plain definition and an irregular-pay option.

    Internal state

    Anxious about misuse, becoming reassured.

    Pain point addressed

    "This month or a normal month" ambiguity; fear of misuse; answers lost on going back.

    N-01N-06T-206T-204
    • Define income as a typical month, with irregular-pay option→no guessing.
    • Show retention period beside the field→lowers fear of misuse.
    • Keep answers when going back→no re-entry of sensitive data.
  3. 3

    Choose plan

    User action

    Compares plans filtered by affordability, each showing due date and missed-payment outcome.

    Internal state

    Cautious, becoming informed.

    Pain point addressed

    Picking the plan that "looked right"; no missed-payment consequences shown.

    N-02T-208
    • Run affordability check before plan options→instalments fit the budget.
    • Align due dates to pay day→fewer early missed payments.
    • Show missed-payment consequences per plan→a decision, not a bet.
    • Stop defaulting to the shortest plan→no "looks right" shortcut.
  4. 4

    Commit or hand off

    User action

    Confirms one plan across all claims, or calls an agent who already sees the context.

    Internal state

    Confident, or wants human reassurance.

    Pain point addressed

    Repeating details to agents; no record of why a plan was offered; late first reminder.

    N-08N-09T-205T-207
    • Confirm one plan across all claims→one clear commitment.
    • Send first-instalment reminder before due date→fewer early misses.
    • Give agents entered data and plan rationale→no repeated questions.

Three advantages over the manual workaround

1. Affordability is calculated in the journey

The consumer no longer rebuilds the check from statements and payslips, where a misjudged "normal" income gives an instalment that is too high.

2. Consequences are visible before commitment

The manual route cannot show what a missed payment triggers. Only an agent call or the chat could, and the chat does not today (T-208).

3. Context travels with the consumer

The manual route ends in a call where the agent starts blank (N-08). The handoff means the call confirms a plan instead of rebuilding it.
